PutItOnYa Is 7-4 and the Math Wants a Word

Let's open with the crime scene. PutItOnYa sits at 7-4, holds the 4th seed, and will probably spend the playoffs acting like it earned this. Meanwhile the all-play record is a gloomy 41-58. That's a +22.2% luck gap, the largest in the league and the kind of number you usually see on a lottery winner's tax return. Their points scored rank 7th. Their record ranks 4th. That is a team that showed up to a potluck with an empty Tupperware and still got seconds.

And look at the points: 1281.8 for, 1284.7 against. PutItOnYa has been outscored on the season and is still 7-4. You have to work at that. Somewhere out there, opposing managers have been auto-benching their studs, oversleeping on waivers, and starting guys on bye weeks, all so this team could pad a record. PutItOnYa, you aren't a contender. You're a witness protection program with a roster. Enjoy the seed. The schedule is about to ask for its money back.
